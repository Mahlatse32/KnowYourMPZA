About Bantu Holomisa

Gen. Holomisa was born on the 25th of July 1955 in the Mqanduli district of Transkei. After attending the Upper Ngqungqu Primary School in Mqanduli, he proceeded to the Jongilizwe College for the Sons of Chiefs and Headmen in Tsolo, Transkei, where he matriculated in 1975. He has served as the Deputy Minister of Environment and Tourism of South Africa in the Government of National Unity (elected in 1994). He co-founded the United Democratic Movement in 1997 with Mr Roelf Meyer and continues to serve as its President. Gen. Holomisa is a fervent advocate for the environment and is working towards the establishment of the Champions of the Environment Foundation. He is married with two children.

Under the Government of National Unity following the 2024 general election, Gen. Holomisa was appointed Deputy Minister of Defence and Military Veterans.

What they said in committee meetings:

What are committee meetings?

Committee meetings are planned events where real-world impactful work happens such as law-making, oversight and public participation - which are all cornerstones of the work our MPs do. Although often citizens focus on the public debate taking place in main chambers, the majority of MPs' time is spent working within committees. Here is a place to see what your committee is saying.

What are plenary appearances?

Plenary sessions are forums that have been created for the purpose of public debate and decision-making. This mechanism is used to convey the messages of our MPs within main chambers on important decisions, like how they vote.

Declaration of interests

What are declaration of interests?

Annually, MPs are required by parliament to register their financial interests and gifts received in their official capacity. Due to the significant influence of the role that MPs take on, there may be times when their personal or business interests become in conflict with the duty prescribed to them as elected officials representing the public interest.
