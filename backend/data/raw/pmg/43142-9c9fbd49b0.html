Committee Reports; Second Term Committee Programme

Presidency

27 May 2026
Chairperson: Ms D Mpapane (ANC)
Share this page:

Meeting Summary

The Presidency Committee sat to consider and adopt its Committee Report on Budget Vote 1: The Presidency, internal Strategic Plan and meeting minutes of meetings held on 4 March, 5 March and 25 March 2026. The Chairperson further proposed an oversight visit in the first term Committee programme before the parliamentary recess, for the Committee to visit provinces that were exemplary in holding their Premiers accountable. Members, however, expressed concern with the proposed time period for such oversight, and the Committee elected to conduct the same in the second term due to Members' availability.

Meeting report

The Chairperson welcomed Members to the meeting. She said Members were aware there was a Question and Answer session in the National Assembly; therefore, it was important for matters to be dealt with promptly so that Members could proceed to the House directly after this meeting. Unfortunately, the Committee did not have a set day and time as of yet for its meetings; therefore, when the time allowed, efforts were made to ensure the Committee met to deal with its business.

She believed all Members received the documents pertaining to today’s business. She recognised there were other Members in attendance standing in for their resident party's full-time Committee Members. She therefore welcomed Mr N Paulsen (EFF), Dr J Hlope (MKP), Dr N Nkabane (ANC), Dr G Koornhof (ANC) and Ms T Siweya (ANC). She explained that the full-time Committee Members were unfortunately held up in other meetings.

She requested that Members move for the adoption of the agenda.

Dr Nkabane moved and Ms K Ramolobeng (ANC) seconded the move. The agenda was duly adopted.

The Chairperson said that following the Committee’s engagement during the strategic planning workshop and the presentation made by the presidency on its strategic plan and Annual Performance Plan (APP) for the financial year 2026/2027, the Committee drafted two reports emanating from the process. Members engaged with the presidency on the tabled plans, including the allocated budget before Parliament.

She added that the Money Bills legislation required National Assembly committees to process the budget allocation and financial targets of departments and entities. Committees fulfilled this responsibility by taking committee reports by vote before the National Assembly. The Committee support staff had prepared the draft reports to further enrich today’s meeting based on the Committee's engagement held on 8 May 2026. Based on the two phases, these documents were intended to guide the Committee on the roadmap it wished to pursue during the remainder of the 7th term of Parliament. These documents were not cast in stone but remained subject to review and refinement during implementation, particularly given that this was a newly established Committee.

The Chairperson requested that apologies be made before proceeding with the presentations.

Mr B Nodada (DA) registered apologies from Mr G Michalakis (DA) and Ms C Labuschagne (DA).

Mr Paulsen registered apologies of Mr J Malema (EFF) and Ms L Mathys (EFF).

Ms Ramolobeng said Mr S Mahumapelo (ANC) was running late, he was in another committee meeting. Further, she apologised on behalf of Mr M Mahlaule (ANC) who was on study leave and Mr M Ntuli (ANC).

Lastly, Dr Hlope registered the apology of Ms SM Mokoena-Zondi (MKP).

The Chairperson registered all apologies and said Dr L Mahlatsi (UAT) had registered his apology via the Committee Secretary. She invited the Content Advisor to proceed.

Mr M Gana (Rise Mzansi) interjected and sought clarity on Members present who were not alternate Members and whether they were standing in for the respective party full-time Committee Members; if not, he was concerned quorum would not be met.

The Chairperson confirmed this had already been discussed, and Members present were co-opted to fill in for the full-time Committee Members. This was done before Mr Gana walked into the meeting. She invited the Content Advisor to proceed and speak to the document because Members would have already perused same.

Report of the Committee on the Presidency on Budget Vote 1: The Presidency

Mr Dustin Davids, Committee Researcher, provided a high-level overview of the report and said he too would take the report as read. He highlighted the key issues, which include the constitutional and legislative mandate of the Committee in performing its oversight, the presidency’s role as the strategic centre of government, discussions had on strengthening policy coordination across government, alignment with the Medium Term Development Plan (MTDP) and priorities of the presidency for the 2026/2027 financial year.

He highlighted that the budget allocation was R816.8 million, compared to R761.6 million from the previous financial year, which was roughly about a 5.9% increased, with the administration programme taking up the bulk of the budget.

He further highlighted matters in relation to the Budget Vote 1, to which the Department had to consider the recommendations made by the Committee.

The Chairperson invited Members to make any comment/additions to the report.

No comment/additions were made.

The Chairperson called for Members to move for the adoption.

Ms Ramolobeng moved for the adoption of the report and Mr Gana seconded the move. The report was duly adopted. See full report here https://pmg.org.za/tabled-committee-report/6656/

Committee Strategic Plan 2026 - 2030

Mr Mnisi, Committee Content Advisor, took the Committee through its strategic plan, outlining the Committee’s directive for the next four years and the plans for implementation for the current year.

As part of its strategic goals, the report highlighted the Committee’s efforts to further enhance and strengthen its oversight mechanisms and public participation to promote an inclusive, transparent and participatory legislative process that would track the voices of citizens, improve integrity and transparency in public office appointments, strengthen international agreements and global positioning.

Stamping oversight over the presidency and across government to accelerate economic reform and service delivery outcomes in order to build a capable, ethical and developmental state.

He highlighted the first strategic goal being to oversee the socio-economic impact system to ensure policy coordination and coherence, delivering annual institutionalised oversight reporting.

The Chairperson called on Members to make inputs and proceed to adopt the report.

Mr Paulsen highlighted the below discrepancies:

- Errors in the period, part of the report indicated different periods, i.e., 2026-2030 and 2025-2030;

- The APP on targets set on the socio-economic impact assessment system were set at 50% yet 80% on the budget report;

- The APP further indicated that international agreements would be reviewed at 80% yet the cumulative targets set per quarter failed to meet the 80% target;

- The APP target set on Operation Vulindlela; and

- State of the Nation Address (SONA) reports target;

He said he would send a list of the discrepancies found to the Committee Secretariat.

Mr S Mahumapelo (ANC) apologised for arriving late. He highlighted a minor comprehension/grammar change in the last paragraph of the report and proceeded to second the move to adopt the report. The report was duly adopted.

Mr Davids proceeded to the next report for consideration which was the Strategic Workshop Report on the workshop held on 8 and 9 May 2026. However, Members had not received a copy thereof. The Committee elected not to proceed with the report and for Members to be provided a copy, and it will be adopted in the next meeting.

Meeting minutes

The Committee considered its meeting minutes on meetings held on 4 March 2026, 5 March 2026 and 25 March 2026. All minutes were duly adopted.

Committee Second Term Programme

The Chairperson took Members through its second term programme. The Committee would be meeting on 2 June 2026 and 3 June 2026 for its budget vote debate. On 26 June 2026, it would be briefed by the presidency on its progress reports on the SONA commitments.

She further proposed that the Committee conduct its oversight/tour visit in a few provinces which were exemplary in holding the office of the Premier accountable. She proposed that such a visit be made a week before the parliamentary recess. She requested Members inputs and asked whether they felt it was too early for such exercise.

Mr A Trollip (Action SA) said it was best to conduct the exercise before the Committee proceeded any further in its work, and it chose provinces which represented best practice in holding Premiers to account. He proposed identifying three/four provinces and Members be furnished with information about them before making a decision on where to visit.

Mr Gana expressed concern with the timing of the oversight visit; the week proposed was the week of 16 June. Further, the weekend thereof was voting registration week, and many Members would be performing their constituency duties. He was concerned Members would already be committed that week and unable to attend the visit. He suggested the oversight be conducted in another week. 

Members concurred with the sentiments made.

The Chairperson said the Committee Secretariat would relook at the programme and the oversight would be conducted in the following term, and proposed that the dates be communicated to the Committee.

Closing Remarks

The Chairperson thanked Members for their engagement and the meeting was adjourned.

Meeting adjourned.
