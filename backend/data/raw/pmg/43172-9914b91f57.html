Election of Chairperson

Impeachment Committee for Section 89 Enquiry

01 June 2026
Share this page:

Meeting Summary

Video

Report of the Section 89 Independent Panel

The Impeachment Committee on the Section 89 Enquiry held its first meeting to elect Mr Makashule Gana (Rise Mzansi) as its Chairperson.

Two members were nominated for the position: Dr Lehlohonolo Mahlatsi (UAT) and Mr Gana. Following a vote by Committee Members, Mr Gana secured the support of 19 Members, while Dr Mahlatsi received 12 votes.

Meeting report

Mr Thembinkosi Ngoma, Committee Secretary, opened the meeting as presiding over the process of electing a Chairperson.

Mr Masibulele Xaso, Secretary to the National Assembly, addressed the Committee, noting that the Speaker of the National Assembly received a letter on 27 May challenging the eligibility of Dr J Hlophe (MKP) to be part of the Committee. He clarified that, currently, there was no impediment in the rules that Dr Hlophe could not form part of the Committee, and therefore the Speaker did not find it necessary to exclude him from proceedings.

Mr M Mahlaule (ANC) accepted the Speaker’s ruling, noting that the ANC found it permissible for Dr Hlophe to participate on the Judicial Services Commission (JSC). While the ANC still held the same view. When the matter was taken to court, Parliament lost. He suggested the Speaker seek legal opinion so that if this [impeachment] process was challenged in court and there was an adverse finding against Parliament, Parliament would be able to demonstrate how it acted in caution and was not found wanting.

Mr Xaso said this would be taken on board, but for the purposes of today, the process will proceed.

Election of Chairperson

Mr Ngoma noted the meeting is called in terms of National Assembly Rule 161 for the purposes of electing a chairperson of the Impeachment Committee on the Section 89 Enquiry. As the Committee does not have the chairperson seated yet, he would facilitate the process of the election of the chairperson. Once the election has concluded, he will hand over to the duly elected chairperson to preside over the meeting.

He explained the procedure to be followed in terms of electing the chairperson. He would call for the nomination, and the Members would raise their hands, be identified, state their name and surname, and then give the full name and surname of the Member being nominated. Once that is done, he would call for a seconder. The seconder will do the same and indicate that he or she seconds the nomination.

Once the nomination has been seconded, he would then proceed to invite the Member so nominated to indicate whether he or she accepts the nomination. If there were one nomination, the matter would not go to a vote. That Member so nominated will be invited to the front, and that Member will be declared the chairperson.

If there were more than one vote, the names of the nominees would be put to a vote by way of a roll call. Members of the Committee who are present will be called by name to indicate their vote, starting with the ANC, followed by the DA, MK, EFF, IFP, PA, Freedom Front Plus, Action SA, ACDP, UDM, Rise Mzansi, BOSA, ATM, Al Jama-ah, NCC, and UAT.

At the end of the vote, the Member with the most votes will then be declared the chairperson of the Impeachment Committee for the Section 89 Enquiry.

The process commenced, and nominations were invited.

Mr Mahlaule nominated Mr M Gana (Rise Mzansi).

This was seconded by Mr G Michalakis (DA).

Mr Gana accepted the nomination.

Ms O Maotwe (EFF) nominated Dr L Mahlatsi (UAT).

This was seconded by Mr N Kwankwa (UDM).

Dr Mahlatsi accepted the nomination.

There were no further nominations.

The matter was taken to a vote:

Votes in favour of Mr Gana: Ms D Mpapane (ANC), Ms D Direko (ANC), Mr X Nqola (ANC), Ms F Muthambi (ANC), Mr C Dugmore (ANC), Mr B Maneli (ANC), Mr Mahlaule, Ms L Makhubela (ANC), Mr Michalakis, Mr B Nodada (DA), Ms K Khakhau (DA), Adv G Breytenbach (DA), Ms N Sharif (DA), Mr H Hadebe (IFP), Mr P Daniels (PA), Mr W Wessels (FF+), Mr Gana, Mr I Ismail-Moosa (Al Jama-ah), and Mr S Lekganyane (ANC) – 19

Votes against Mr Gana: Dr Hlophe, Mr S Gama (MKP), Dr K Litchfield-Tshabalala (MKP), Mr J Malema (EFF), Ms Maotwe, Ms L Ngobeni (Action SA), Mr S Swart (ACDP), Mr Kwankwa, Dr M Maimane (BOSA), Mr V Zungula (ATM), Mr F Adams (NCC), Dr Mahlatsi – 12

Votes against Dr Mahlatsi: Ms Mpapane, Ms Direko, Mr Lekganyane, Mr Nqola, Ms Muthambi, Mr Dugmore, Mr Maneli, Mr Mahlaule, Ms Makhubela, Mr Michalakis, Mr Nodada, Ms Khakhau, Adv Breytenbach, Ms Sharif, Mr Hadebe, Mr Daniels, Mr Wessels, Mr Gana, Mr Ismail-Moosa - 19

Votes in favour of Dr Mahlatsi: Dr Hlophe, Mr Gama, Dr Litchfield-Tshabalala, Mr Malema, Ms Maotwe, Ms Ngobeni, Mr Swart, Mr Kwankwa, Dr Maimane, Mr Zungula, Mr Adams, Dr Mahlatsi – 12

A few moments were taken for the staff to verify the votes.

The Committee Secretary confirmed that Mr Gana had received the majority of votes.

Mr Gana was thus declared the newly elected Chairperson of the Impeachment Committee on Section 89.

The Chairperson addressed the Committee. He thanked Members for the nomination and participating in the process, noting that the work of the Committee was now in progress and had just begun. He would ensure the Committee did that which was in its control.

He added that there are processes on the go regarding the rules, but this would not stop the work of the Committee; they would work in parallel. He thanked the staff, noting he would communicate with Members on the next processes.

He adjourned the meeting.
