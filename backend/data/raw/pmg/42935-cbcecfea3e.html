Election of NA Co-Chairperson

Joint Committee on Ethics and Members’ Interest

08 May 2026
Chairperson: Mr J Britz (DA, Eastern Cape)
Share this page:

Meeting Summary

The Joint Committee on Ethics and Members' Interests met to elect a new co-chairperson for the National Assembly component following the redeployment of the previous co-chairperson. One nomination, Ms Mvana, was accepted, while another nominee, Adv Breytenbach, declined due to existing committee responsibilities. Ms Mvana was therefore duly elected as co-chairperson.

Meeting report

The Committee noted the apologies from Mr M Billy (DA, KZN), Ms S Nxumalo (ANC, Mpumalanga), Mr J Malema (EFF), Mr M Gana (Rise Mzansi) and Mr N Hadebe (IFP).

Co-Chairperson Britz indicated that the Committee's previous co-chairperson for the National Assembly (NA) component was deployed to another committee position, and the Committee hence needed to elect a co-chairperson.

Adv Anthea Gordon, Registrar of Members' Interests, indicated that the meeting for the election is an open process and is open to the public.

Ms N Mvana (ANC) was sworn in to replace the previous co-chairperson’s membership position.

Mr O Mathafa (ANC) nominated Ms N Mvana (ANC) to be the co-chairperson.

Mr B Farmer (PA, Western Cape) seconded that nomination.

Ms Mvana accepted the nomination.

Mr H Van den Berg (FF+, Northern Cape) nominated Adv G Breytenbach (DA) to be the co-Chairperson.

However, Adv Breytenbach politely declined, citing that she was co-chairing another committee and was thus unable to accept the nomination.

With no further nominations, Ms Mvana was duly elected as the co-chairperson for the National Assembly component of the Committee.

Mr Farmer indicated that the meeting agenda before Members was tabled in the absence of the NA co-chairperson, rendering the process null and void. He suggested that this meeting not continue.

Adv Gordon explained that decisions of the meeting will take effect after the co-Chairperson has been elected.

Chairperson Britz concurred with Adv Gordon's input.

The Chairperson adjourned the meeting.
